The Forma Sneaker

Concept Release · Details coming soon

The Forma Sneaker synthesizes architectural lines with ergonomic equilibrium. Defined by sculpted outsole contours, low-profile collar articulation, and subtle geometric overlays, it is engineered for effortless transition through metropolitan landscapes.

Select Size (EU) EU 42
Notice: Purchase functionality will be connected soon. Register to receive priority release scheduling and allocation notifications.
Constructed around an asymmetric last that mirrors natural foot displacement. Clean paneling minimizes visual distraction while providing structured lateral stability for walking postures.
Precision layered textile upper combined with composite rubber tread formulation. Dense woven heel pull and reinforced eyelet construction.
Standard European unisex sizing. The ergonomic toe box offers relaxed volume while the midfoot remains comfortably locked.
Delivery timetables, regional logistics, and archival presentation packaging will be formally communicated upon release inauguration.
